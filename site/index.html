npx create-open-logs

Deploys a Worker and a D1 database. Then sign in with the admin password it prints.

Live tail

Every event as it lands. Search text or status:>=500, click a bar to zoom, j/k to move.

Charts without SQL

Count, count over time, average or p95 of any field. Bar, pie, line or a big number, pinned to a dashboard.

Team sign-in

Invite teammates by email with owner, admin or member roles. Sessions live in your D1.

A token per source

Each app gets its own ingest token, shown once and stored hashed. Rejected requests surface in the UI.

Honest freshness

On Basin, data is 1–3 minutes behind. The tail tells you exactly how far, instead of pretending.

Plain HTTP in

One POST /v1/logs with JSON. No SDK needed: curl, fetch, Python or Go in a few lines.

How it works

  1. Ingest. Your apps send JSON to /v1/logs with a source token. The Worker validates it and stamps the project and source.
  2. Store. Small installs keep logs in D1. At volume, switch to Basin: Pipelines stream them into an Iceberg table in R2.
  3. Query. The UI sends structured filters, never SQL. One builder turns them into parameterised queries for D1 or Basin SQL.

What it costs

Open Logs is free. You pay Cloudflare for what you use on your own account.

D1 storage (default)
Works on the Workers free plan for small teams and low volume.
Basin storage
Needs Workers Paid ($5/month minimum) plus Basin usage. Logs are 1–3 minutes behind.
